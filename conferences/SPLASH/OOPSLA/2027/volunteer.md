# Volunteer registration is now open!  Register here!

Splash! at Cornell is a one-day in-person education program where college students teach students any topics of their liking. All activities will be facilitated by Cornell students. And, because of that, we need volunteers! Volunteers will play a crucial role in enabling teachers to deliver a seamless program and provide students with a conducive learning environment.

Below are some details for this year’s event:

*   When: Saturday, November 21st, 2026, from 10 AM - 5 PM (ET)
*   Volunteers help with program setup, registration, lunch, and clean-up
*   **You must complete the Cornell Child & Youth Safety forms by November 6th**
*   You get free lunch and a t-shirt!
*   Service hours can be provided!

If you would also like to moderate classes (sitting in a class, taking attendance, and ensuring the class runs smoothly), you can also sign up to volunteer and email us to let us know your preference. Moderators are also required to complete the Child & Youth Safety forms and a background check.

We also encourage you to join our volunteer listserv by emailing us at splashcornell@gmail.com.

Thank you!
