LICS 2027

Forty-Second Annual Symposium on
 Logic in Computer Science (LICS)
 Montreal • 21–24 June 2027

* * *

Montreal from Mount Royal (photo by Diego Delso, CC BY-SA 4.0).

The LICS Symposium is an annual international forum on theoretical and practical topics in computer science that relate to logic, broadly construed. LICS 2027 will be hosted in Montreal, Canada during 21–24 June 2027, with workshops on 19–20 June.
