# ICFP 2027

The ACM SIGPLAN International Conference on Functional Programming (ICFP) is the main international forum for research, education, and practice in functional programming. ICFP covers the full range of work on functional programming languages, from theory and design to implementation, applications, and experience.

ICFP 2027 will take place in Nijmegen, The Netherlands, from Sunday 26 September until Friday 1 October, 2027. The main conference will take place on Monday, Tuesday Wednesday; co-located events will take place on Sunday, Thursday, Friday.

ICFP 2027 is planned as an in-person conference. Talks of the main conference will be recorded and placed on Youtube, remote presentations will be available for authors who cannot attend in person due to unavoidable reasons (e.g., travel restrictions). We do not plan to have recordings of co-located events.
